# Demo script: 10 minutes

Exact taps for the manufacturer demo. Set everything up first with `docs/setup-guide.md`.

**Before you start**
- Three phones (or browsers), each logged in and on the home screen:
  - **Phone M:** the Manufacturer;
  - **Phone B:** Buyer 1, in the distributor's district (Satara);
  - **Phone D:** the Distributor.
- Buyer 2 (another district, e.g. Kolhapur) on a fourth phone, or log Phone B out and in as Buyer 2 for step 3.
- Yesterday's rates were published (setup step 9), and a follow-up is due today (setup step 10).

---

## 1. The manufacturer moves every rate by +₹300 and publishes (2 minutes)

**Phone M**
1. Tap **Publish today's rates**. Yesterday's rates are filled in.
2. Under **Move every rate by**, keep **+**, type `300` and tap **Apply**.
3. Tap **Yes, move**. "Every rate moved by +₹300" appears, and each box shows yesterday's rate plus 300.
4. Tap **Next: check the rates**. Each district is folded, showing its difference and how many rates it holds. Tap one district to show the final rate for every grade and size.
5. Tap **Confirm and publish**. "Published. Buyers now see your new rates." appears, and the rates now show as locked.

*Say:* "Four taps each morning, one publish, and every buyer sees only his own final rate."

## 2. A buyer in the Type B district sees the new rate, with the tag (1 minute)

**Phone B** (Buyer 1, Satara)
1. Tap **Rates in my area**.
2. Show the manufacturer's card:
   - the new rate, 300 higher;
   - **Updated [time]** in large type;
   - **Authorised distributor for Satara: [distributor]**.

*Say:* "He sees his Satara rate only, never the basic rate or the differences."

## 3. A buyer in another district sees a different rate (1 minute)

**Buyer 2** (e.g. Kolhapur)
1. Tap **Rates in my area**.
2. Show the same grade and size: a different figure, labelled with his own district.

*Say:* "One publish; each district gets its own rate automatically."

## 4. The buyer sends an enquiry; the distributor receives it (2 minutes)

**Phone B** (Buyer 1)
1. On the manufacturer's card, tap **Send enquiry**.
2. Choose a grade and a size, type a quantity in tonnes and a short message.
3. Leave **Show my name and phone to the seller** ticked, then tap **Send enquiry**. "Enquiry sent." appears.

**Phone D** (Distributor)
1. Tap **Enquiries received**.
2. Show the enquiry: grade, size, quantity, message, the buyer's area, name and **Call** link, and "Via [manufacturer] profile".

*Say:* "In his appointed district, the enquiry goes straight to the authorised distributor."

## 5. The manufacturer's client book (3 minutes)

**Phone M**
1. Go back to the home screen and tap **Client book**. **Today's follow-ups** shows the follow-up due today (an overdue one shows in red).
2. On that follow-up, tap **Call**. The phone's dialer opens; end the call, then come back to the app.
3. ALEE asks **Next follow-up**, saying one follow-up was closed. Tap **No next follow-up** for now.
4. On the customer's page, open **Log a visit**, type what happened (e.g. "Visited site, order next week") and tap **Log a visit**.
5. ALEE asks again. Choose a date next week, then tap **Save next follow-up**.
6. On the customer's page, show **Activity**: the call and the visit, with times. Then show **Follow-ups**: next week's follow-up waiting.

*Say:* "His own customers, private to him. Every call and visit is recorded and can't be changed, and ALEE never lets a follow-up drop."

---

## If something goes wrong

- **A buyer sees "Rate not available" or "Price on request":** that buyer's district isn't set up for the manufacturer (setup step 6).
- **No "Authorised distributor" tag:** the distributor hasn't accepted (setup step 8), or isn't approved (setup step 2).
- **The enquiry says "Received after close":** the closing time has passed (setup step 7).
- **The publish screen shows locked rates:** today's rates are already out. Tap **Change today's rates**.
- **A page won't load on one phone:** check that phone's internet, then pull down to reload. If it still fails, switch it between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
